**Title**
TasteBridge Groups: the taste diplomat for real-life plans (Qloo Agentic Hackathon)

**Description**
Planning a night out for 2 to 8 people usually ends in an averaged, forgettable compromise, or the loudest voice wins.
TasteBridge Groups asks Qloo's Taste AI instead: it resolves everyone's tastes into real Qloo entities, finds where they conflict, and bridges those conflicts with real venues, scored for fairness. Every stop explains itself, and anything ruled out (closed at that hour, a hard no) says why.

What stands out
- Conflicts are bridged, not averaged: five conflict axes, a scored conflict penalty, and a personal win for every guest
- It shows its work: ruled-out venues with reasons, opening hours checked against Qloo's own data
- Live agent loop: plan, resolve, retrieve, reflect and retry, score, synthesize
- With vs without Qloo, side by side
- One-click rebalance, CLI, MCP server (plan_group_outing), 72 tests, and a 60-group evaluation

Live demo: https://tastebridge-groups.onrender.com
